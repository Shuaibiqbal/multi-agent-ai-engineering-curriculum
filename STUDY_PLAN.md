# Study Plan — 2 Topics a Day (Sunday to Friday)

_This file is made by `tools/build_study_plan.py` from the real documents. Do not edit it by hand — run the script again when the documents change._

## How to use this plan

- **Every study day (Sunday to Friday) has 2 items.** Do them in order. One item is about 1 to 2 hours of focused work.
- **For a topic:** click the link, read the topic slowly, and write 3 to 5 lines of notes in your own words. When a topic is the first one of a new document, first read that document's short "The Story" part at the top.
- **For practice:** try the exercise by yourself first. Use Hint 1, then Hint 2, only after you really tried. Look at the solution last.
- **When you finish an item, tick it off:** change `☐` to `☑` in the Done? column.
- **Saturday is not a new-topic day.** It is the revision and catch-up day. The Saturday row lists every item of that week again, with its link. Open each one, read it quickly, and check that you still understand it. Also finish anything you did not finish.
- **If you fall behind,** do not skip items. Just move forward from where you are — the dates are a guide, not a rule.

## How items were chosen

- **Topics:** every `###` topic inside a document's **Core Concepts** part is 1 item. Documents with no Core Concepts (like Doc15, Doc17, Doc18) use their main reading parts instead — each one is 1 item (a part with sub-parts is split into 1 item per sub-part).
- **Practice exercises:** after a document's topics, its practice exercises are grouped **2 exercises per item**.
- **Build Task:** a document's Build Task takes **2 items** (build it, then check it with its Test Cases). When a document says "You Can Now Build Project N", its Build Task is only a summary of that project, so it is not scheduled twice — you build the project instead.
- **Projects:** a project starts right after the document that unlocks it (from the Project ↔ Document map in [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md), or the document's own "You Can Now Build Project N" part). Its Setup is 1 item, and every Step is **2 items** (build it, then test it). Main projects come before bonus projects.
- **Bonus projects** are included. They are optional: if you skip one, just continue with the next row and your dates move earlier.

**In total:** 316 items = 122 topics + 57 practice items + 16 Build Task items + 121 project items, over **158 study days** in **27 weeks** (from 20 Sep 2026 to 27 Mar 2027).
